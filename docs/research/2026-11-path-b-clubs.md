# Selling Siroko Club Desk to other schools: research (2026-11-23)

Step 1 of path B: find schools, see what they use today, prepare outreach.
**Caveat:** the session's network blocked the schools' own websites. Booking
methods come from search results and third-party listings only; "no booking
tool found" is unverified for every school.

## Summary

- **34 schools found:** 19 in Bulgaria, 15 in Romania / Greece / Turkey.
- **Almost nobody uses booking software.** Bulgaria: none found. Region: one
  chain (KBC Limnos) has full online booking. The rest run on phone,
  WhatsApp/Facebook, email, walk-ins, plus outside channels (Expedia and other
  OTAs, KiteJungle/Manawa, gift vouchers, the Multisport card, resort
  concessions).
- **Schools are small and seasonal** (about June to September), many with
  under 25 reviews. Several also train instructors and run rental fleets, so
  instructor pay and rental tracking matter more than a booking widget.
- **Own club location:** public listings put Siroko Wind Club on **Keros
  beach, Limnos, Greece**. Surf Club Keros, Duotone Pro Center Limnos and KBC
  Limnos (regional list) are therefore neighbours. Selling to them is the
  owner's call.

**Room for Siroko Desk:** Bulgarian (and Greek / Romanian) language, instructor
payroll built in, mobile-first, flat **€40–100/month**, below Bookinglayer and
cheaper than Viking's 3% for any school with more than ~€30k in yearly
bookings.

## Before selling: product gaps

The README says the app is single-club, online mode is read-only, and team
logins are "the next step". Selling needs multiple clubs, sign-in for each
club's staff, data separated by club, and the club's own price list and
payroll rules.

## Interview questions (for the 15-minute call)

1. How many instructors, lessons per day in peak season, rental items?
2. What do you use today for bookings, lesson schedule, rentals, payments?
   (paper, Excel/Google Sheets, WhatsApp, Bookinglayer/Viking/FareHarbor...)
3. What is the most annoying or time-consuming part of running the season?
4. How do you calculate instructor pay (hourly, % per lesson, per student)?
   How long does it take each month? Have there been disputes?
5. Do customers book online today? Through which channels (site, Booking,
   GetYourGuide, Instagram)?
6. Have you tried any booking software? Why did you keep or drop it?
7. If one app did [their pain], what would that be worth per month?
   (Listen first; then test €40 / €70 / €100 per month.)
8. Who in the club would decide to buy, and when do you prepare for the season?
9. Can we follow up in [month] with a demo? Would you try it free in 2027?

**Pass rule (proposed):** at least 10 conversations, and at least 5 clubs say
they would pay €40+/month or agree to a free pilot with a named start date.
